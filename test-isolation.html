Fullsize — hostile host-page fixture

Dev-only. Not part of the shipped extension. This page attacks the injected UI with universal !important rules, button and div element rules, a positioned and offset body, a near-maximum z-index overlay, click-stealing wrappers, and a script that rewrites every button on the page after load.

The whole test is one question: does the Save control look and behave here exactly as it does on test-page.html? Open both side by side. If this page's CSS is reaching our button you will see it immediately — hot pink, Comic Sans, dashed red border, or wildly wrong position.

1. Baseline under attack

An ordinary image on a hostile page. Expect: the normal small dark "Save ▾" pill, correct font, correctly placed at the image's top-right — not pink, not Comic Sans, not shifted by the body's 40px border or its centring margin.

2. Click stealing

The image sits inside a link, wrapped by a container whose capture-phase handler calls stopPropagation() and preventDefault() on every click, mousedown and mouseover. A lightbox would look like this. Expect: the button still appears, the save still runs, and the log below stays empty — the page must never see our click, and we must never trigger its navigation.

3. Narrow image

120px wide — narrower than the control itself. Expect: the bar pins to the image's LEFT edge rather than hanging off into the margin.

4. Flush against the right viewport edge

Expect: the control stays fully on screen — clamped inward, never clipped off the right edge or triggering a horizontal scrollbar.

5. Picker near the bottom of the window

Scroll so this image sits at the very bottom, then open the ▾ menu. Expect: the menu is clamped upward so all three items stay visible, rather than running off the bottom.
